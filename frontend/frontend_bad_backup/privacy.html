Welcome to SocialSphere

SocialSphere is a social media platform that allows users to create accounts, publish posts, upload media, communicate with other users, like and comment on content, follow accounts and use other social features.

By using SocialSphere, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

🔐

Protected

We use reasonable security practices to protect account information.

👤

Your Control

You can manage your profile, posts and other information available through your account.

🚫

No Password Sharing

Your password should never be publicly displayed or shared with other users.

1

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly when creating or using your SocialSphere account.

Account Information

  • Name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password in securely hashed form
  • Profile picture
  • Biography and profile information

Content You Create

When you use SocialSphere, we may store content that you voluntarily upload or create, including:

  • Posts
  • Photos
  • Videos and reels
  • Comments
  • Likes
  • Stories
  • Messages
  • Profile information

Usage Information

We may automatically receive technical information necessary for operating, protecting and improving the platform, such as browser information, device information, timestamps and application activity.

2

How We Use Your Information

Information collected through SocialSphere may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Authenticate you when you log in.
  • Display your profile and posts.
  • Provide likes, comments, follows, messages and notifications.
  • Allow users to search for and interact with accounts.
  • Provide account recovery and password reset functionality.
  • Detect abuse, spam or suspicious activity.
  • Improve platform performance and user experience.
  • Maintain platform security.
3

Your Posts and Public Content

Information you intentionally publish through SocialSphere may be visible to other users depending on the features and privacy controls available on your account.

Publicly visible information may include your username, profile picture, biography, posts, reels, comments, follower information and other activity.

Remember Information that you publish publicly may be viewed, copied or shared by other people. Consider this before posting personal or sensitive information.
4

How Information May Be Shared

SocialSphere does not intentionally sell your personal account information.

Information may nevertheless be shared in limited situations necessary for operating the platform.

  • With other users when you intentionally publish public content.
  • With infrastructure or hosting providers used to operate the platform.
  • When required to protect users, prevent fraud or address security problems.
  • When required to comply with applicable legal obligations.
5

Account and Data Security

SocialSphere uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect user information.

Passwords should be stored using secure password hashing rather than as plain text.

However, no website, application, database or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security.

You can help protect your account by:

  • Using a strong and unique password.
  • Never sharing your password.
  • Logging out from shared devices.
  • Keeping your email account secure.
  • Reporting suspicious account activity.
6

Cookies and Sessions

SocialSphere may use cookies, authentication tokens or similar browser storage technologies to keep users signed in and maintain secure sessions.

These technologies can also be used to remember certain settings and provide features necessary for the website to function correctly.

7

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the features available on SocialSphere, you may be able to:

  • Edit your profile information.
  • Change your profile image.
  • Delete your posts.
  • Remove comments.
  • Unfollow users.
  • Remove saved posts.
  • Change or reset your password.
  • Request deletion of your account where that functionality is provided.
8

Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide SocialSphere services, maintain accounts, protect platform security and comply with applicable obligations.

Content deleted by a user may require a limited period of time to disappear completely from backups or technical systems.

9

Third-Party Services

SocialSphere may depend on third-party infrastructure or services for features such as database hosting, website hosting, email delivery, storage or authentication support.

Those providers may process limited information necessary to provide their services.

10

Children's Privacy

Users should only use SocialSphere if they meet the minimum age required by applicable laws and the platform's terms.

If information is discovered to have been collected from a person who is not legally permitted to use the service, appropriate action may be taken to remove the account and associated information.

11

Changes to This Privacy Policy

SocialSphere may update this Privacy Policy when features, security practices or legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date displayed on this page may be revised.

Continued use of SocialSphere after an updated policy becomes effective means that you acknowledge the updated policy.

Questions About Privacy?

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact SocialSphere through the contact page.
